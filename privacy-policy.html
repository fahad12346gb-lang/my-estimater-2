Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

My Estimateer (“we”, “our”, or “the service”) provides construction-estimate tools. This policy explains how information may be handled when you use the website.

Information you provide

You may enter project information such as country, city/location, building type, area, floors, construction quality, and estimate details. If you use the drawing/plan feature, you may upload an image or document for analysis.

How information is used

Project information is used to calculate and display estimates, provide requested AI assistance, save estimates in your browser when the history feature is used, and operate paid features.

Payments

Payments are processed by the selected payment provider. Payment credentials are handled by the provider according to its policies.

Cookies, storage and advertising

The website may use browser storage for preferences and estimate history. If advertising is enabled, third-party advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies.

Data retention

Information stored locally by the history feature remains in your browser until you delete it or clear relevant site data. Information handled by connected services may be retained according to those services’ policies.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the service changes. The latest version will be published on this page.
